---
title: Red teaming and safety tests
---
Red teaming is adversarial testing by people or automated generators who try to make the system misbehave: produce harmful content, leak personal data, take unauthorised actions or give confident wrong advice in high-stakes areas.

Turn findings into a permanent safety suite. Typical categories are jailbreaks, data exfiltration through tools, personal data leakage, unsafe advice in medical, legal or financial topics, and bias across demographic slices.

Define clear pass criteria per category, such as refusal plus a safe alternative, or escalation to a human. Re-run the suite after every model or prompt change, because safety behaviour can regress silently.
